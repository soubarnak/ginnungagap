# S-05s: create-time forms of ADR-007 mitigations a+b+d (NFR-003)

Date: 2026-10-14. Host gate, run by the implementation agent on the reference host (Void Linux glibc x86_64). Nothing committed.

## Verdict

- **Criterion 1, journald honours the mounted drop-in: PASS.** The read-only bind of `ginn.conf` is read as config, the runtime journal's computed maximum is 8 MiB, and nothing lands in `/var/log/journal`.
- **Criterion 2, idle median within ±1 MiB of 37.63 MiB: FAIL.** The a+b+d median is **35.96 MiB** (10 of 10 counted, none excluded). The band is 36.63 to 38.63 MiB, so the median is 0.67 MiB below its lower edge and 1.67 MiB below the reference. The result without the two flagged low-RssFile rows is 36.48 MiB, still 0.15 MiB below the edge. The verdict does not depend on that exclusion.
- **Criterion 3, masks take effect: PASS.** All ten units in `spikes/s05/masks-base.txt` print `LoadState=masked` and `ActiveState=inactive`, in the trial and in the first counted attempt.

**Overall: FAIL on criterion 2.** Under the roadmap rule, T7.3 does not ship the a+b+d forms until S-05s passes or CL-10 is decided. That decision belongs to the orchestrator. The a+b+d realm is well inside the NFR-003 budget of 40 MiB (median 35.96, max 37.35). The failure is against the S-05r reference band, not against the budget.

## Criterion 1: journald honours the drop-in (PASS)

Evidence, from the trial probe and the measured-attempt probe (same content in both):

- `systemd-analyze cat-config systemd/journald.conf` lists `/etc/systemd/journald.conf.d/ginn.conf` after the vendor defaults, with `Storage=volatile` and `RuntimeMaxUse=8M`.
- `findmnt` on the drop-in: `ro,noatime,commit=15 /dev/nvme0n1p2[.../spikes/s05/journald-ginn.conf] /etc/systemd/journald.conf.d/ginn.conf`. Writing to it: `touch: cannot touch '/etc/systemd/journald.conf.d/ginn.conf': Read-only file system`.
- journald's computed maximum, from the journal's own start line, survives `SYSTEMD_LOG_LEVEL=warning`:
  `systemd-journald[15]: Runtime Journal (/run/log/journal/6951dcb0dee6419ba94cc0ca0f3265e0) is 1M, max 8M, 7M free.`
- Base control, same host and image, no drop-in: `Runtime Journal (...) is 8M, max 759.5M, 751.5M free.` The drop-in changes the computed maximum from 759.5 MiB to 8 MiB.
- `ls /run/log/journal /var/log/journal`: the journal is under `/run/log/journal`. `/var/log/journal` exists and is empty.
- `journalctl --disk-usage` in the trial: 1M. Journal lines since boot: 6.

The behavioural cap check (12 MB pushed through `systemd-cat`) was not run, because the max line was present in the trial. The trial's `podman diff` shows `A /etc/systemd/journald.conf.d/ginn.conf`, which is podman's mountpoint stub, not a ginn write (CR-007, ADR-007 Decision).

## Caveats

- One realm, one host, one image. No second distro.
- The kernel changed from `7.2.9_1` to `7.2.10_1`, and the baseline control also moved (about 0.8 MiB). The a+b+d gap to S-05r cannot be attributed to the mounts alone.
- The argv keeps S-05r's flags and does not add `--stop-timeout 30` (ADR-003). ADR-007 says idle RSS does not depend on it, and that remains unmeasured.
- The base control was an addition to the roadmap procedure. It is labelled as such and is not part of the pass criteria.
- Sample count: 10 counted per config, 60 s idle, no exclusions in either config, as S-05r.
- `spikes/s05/s05s-mem.tsv` is not committed. The script's per-sample memory is in `s05s-on-samples.tsv` and `s05s-run.log`.

## Deviations from the roadmap entry

1. **One root command.** The roadmap entry does not mention root. The launch leaf needs the one root write, as S-05r did. It is logged above, and it was used only after the unprivileged write was refused.
2. **Base control sweep added** (10 rows, labelled `on-base`).
3. **No layered image.** The `:ab` image of S-05r was not built. The mitigations are create-time mounts and environment only. `localhost/ginn-s05s:base` is the only image.
4. **Drop-in source.** The bind source is the committed file `spikes/s05/journald-ginn.conf`, not a copy in the spike dir. The target name is `ginn.conf`.
5. **Cap check not run.** The journald max line was present in the trial, so the behavioural cap check was not needed.
6. **Mask check in the trial and the first counted attempt of each config**, as the orchestrator brief asked.
7. **measure.py.** One change: `SPIKE_XRD_TAIL` became `SPIKE_XRD_TAILS`, so the `s05s` spike dir is accepted. S-05r's dir is still accepted, and nothing else changed.
8. **`check` and `prepare` ran once before the leg**, outside the leaf and without root. The leg then ran them again. The second `prepare` reused the build cache.

## What it unblocks

- **Criterion 1 passes**: the read-only drop-in works as a create-time mount, and the computed maximum is 8 MiB.
- **Criterion 3 passes**: the create-time unit masks take effect.
- **Criterion 2 fails**: the a+b+d median is 35.96 MiB, outside the 36.63 to 38.63 band. Under the roadmap rule, T7.3 must not ship the a+b+d forms until S-05s passes or CL-10 is decided. This result does not decide CL-10. The orchestrator decides whether to accept the new reference, repeat the measurement, or fall back to CL-10.
- NFR-003 against the 40 MiB budget is not failed by this run. The a+b+d median (35.96) and max (37.35) are both under 40.
